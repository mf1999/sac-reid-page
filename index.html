    Overview

    The dataset contains image crops of vessels in St. Anthony's Channel near Šibenik, Croatia. One fixed camera recorded the footage at 1920×1080 pixels and 25 frames per second. Each crop shows one vessel. Crops of the same vessel have the same identity number (pid).

    The footage originates from the Martinska Research Station, part of the Ruđer Bošković Institute.

    • Train and val: automatic detection and tracking in the footage, then human review of each identity.
    • Test: the hand-annotated ground-truth boxes of 59 multi-object tracking sequences of the same camera.

    No identity is in two splits. The layout is the Market-1501 layout. A file name is <pid:6>_c<camid:2>_f<frame:6>[_m<k>].jpg, and crops.csv gives the box, the split, the unit and the recording time of each crop.

    Evaluation

    A standard Market-1501 evaluator gives scores that are too high on this dataset, because the gallery holds near-duplicate crops of the same track. The benchmark protocol counts a match only after a time gap and a position change. Use val to select models and settings, and use test only for the final result. The README in the archive gives the full protocol.

    Privacy

    No person is identifiable in the footage. No registration number is clearly visible, except on the large state-owned ferries and the fast passenger boats.

    Download

    The dataset is on Zenodo with restricted access. Zenodo sends the files after the author approves the request.

    1. Open the Zenodo record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24015510
    2. Select Request access and give your name, your affiliation and the purpose of your research.
    3. Accept the CC BY-NC 4.0 license. Commercial use is not permitted.

    The archive SaintAnthonysChannelReID_v1.0.zip contains the four image folders, crops.csv, README.md, LICENSE.txt, CITATION.cff and version.json. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24015510. Release date: 2026-11-06.
